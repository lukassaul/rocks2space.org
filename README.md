# Rocks2Space website

Public-facing website for **Rocks2Space**, an open, interdisciplinary research initiative. The research and strategy documents live separately in [rocks-to-space](https://github.com/lukassaul/rocks-to-space).

## Site

The site is a lightweight static website with no build step or third-party runtime dependencies. Open `index.html` locally to preview it. Edit `index.html`, `styles.css`, and `script.js` to update the site.

## Publish with GitHub Pages

1. Open this repository's **Settings → Pages**.
2. Under **Build and deployment**, select **Deploy from a branch**.
3. Select branch **main**, folder **/(root)**, and save.
4. Wait for the Pages deployment to finish. The initial preview URL will be similar to `https://lukassaul.github.io/rocks2space.org/`.
5. When ready to connect the purchased domain, enter **rocks2space.org** under **Custom domain** in Pages settings, then follow GitHub's DNS instructions at your registrar. Configure both apex and www as appropriate. Wait for DNS and the certificate to provision, then enable **Enforce HTTPS**.

**Important:** Do not assume the domain is connected just because this repository exists. DNS and Pages configuration are separate manual steps. Consider GitHub's domain verification feature to protect your domain.

## Editorial guidelines

- Distinguish established evidence from hypotheses and long-range aspirations.
- Emphasize practical research benefits and interdisciplinary collaboration.
- Treat planetary protection and responsible governance as central, not optional.
- Do not claim a legal nonprofit status, funder, partnership, or contact address unless confirmed.
- Keep technical details and citations in the research repository; link to them from the public site.

## Future enhancements

Add a contact channel once established, researcher contribution guidelines, event listings, news, and accessible imagery as the community develops.
