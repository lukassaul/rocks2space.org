WHY ROCKS2SPACE EXISTS

Life is precious.
Its survival is not guaranteed.

Earth is the only world known to support life. Yet our biosphere faces urgent threats today, from climate change and biodiversity loss to the possibility of catastrophic events. Over astronomical timescales, the Sun's evolution will also end Earth's capacity to sustain the life we know.

The survival of life cannot be taken for granted. What we do now matters.

Rocks2Space investigates whether life could endure beyond its planet of origin. Our purpose is not to abandon Earth, but to protect and nurture its biosphere while exploring how life's story might continue beyond its birthplace.

OUR MISSION

A bold question.
Useful science at every step.

Rocks2Space explores panspermia, lithopanspermia, biological resilience, the limits of Earth's habitability, and the physical pathways by which material might move between planetary systems.

Our guiding principle is dual benefit: every stage of investigation should produce valuable scientific, technological, educational, or societal results—even if the most distant possibilities remain unproven or unreachable.

We welcome rigorous critique and participation from researchers who do not share every long-term aspiration.

HOW WE WORK

Exploration with integrity.

Evidence before certainty

Separate established results, testable hypotheses, and speculation. Publish uncertainty and negative findings.

Earth first

Protect Earth's biosphere and respect planetary protection, biosafety, and international governance.

Open across disciplines

Invite independent scrutiny and collaborations across geology, biology, aerospace, computing, and the humanities.
