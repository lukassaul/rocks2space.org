## Using the graphics

- **Outreach and promotion:** Use art to illustrate Rocks2Space's questions and vision; do not imply that interstellar biological dispersal has been demonstrated.
- **Research proposals:** Label artistic concepts as illustrations, not experimental results, mission designs, or scientific evidence.
- **Education:** Explain that mushroom spores and flower pollen traveling among stars are visual metaphors. Rocks2Space's core research concerns possible transport of life-bearing **rocks**.
- **Earth stewardship:** The Earth flag is an illustrative design, not an internationally adopted or official flag.
- **Attribution and licensing:** Do not assume all files have a blanket open license. Confirm the applicable rights and licensing terms before redistribution, especially for third-party additions. Record the creator, source, creation date, and any restrictions for future contributions.

## Suggested archive organization

- `graphics/originals/` — full-resolution standalone artwork.
- `graphics/concepts/` — website mockups and exploratory compositions.
- `graphics/exports/` — resized or cropped derivatives for slides, social media, and print.

When adding an image, include a descriptive filename, its purpose, a short alt-text description, and a note identifying whether it is artistic, diagrammatic, or data-derived.

The homepage currently uses the SVG illustrations under `assets/`. The original photorealistic images remain preferred candidates for a future homepage revision once their full-resolution PNG files are committed.
