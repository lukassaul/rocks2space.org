# Interstellar probe mission concepts and researcher index

**Rocks2Space research reference — working bibliography (2026-11-17)**

## Purpose and scope

Collect historical and contemporary interstellar probe proposals relevant to the **delivery** of payloads between stars. Record original studies, conference proceedings/abstracts, researchers, and engineering constraints. Inclusion **does not imply** feasibility, biological suitability, or endorsement of directed panspermia.

Distinguish **interstellar precursor missions** (exploration of the heliosphere boundary/local interstellar medium) from **star-to-star probes** (intended to reach another stellar system). Conference abstracts must be identified as such, not mistaken for peer-reviewed articles.

### NASA primary reports: Project Longshot

**1988 design report** — *Project Longshot: An Unmanned Probe to Alpha Centauri*; NASA-CR-184718; NASA NTRS document [19890007533](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19890007533). The NASA record lists **Keith A. Beals, Martin Beaulieu, Frank J. Dembia, Joseph Kerstiens, Daniel L. Kramer, Jeffrey R. West and James A. Zito**. The concept involves a fusion-propelled Alpha Centauri mission and a roughly century-long transit. **Type:** NASA contractor report; **category:** true interstellar probe.

**1989 follow-up** — *Project Longshot: A Mission to Alpha Centauri*; NASA-CR-186052; NASA NTRS document [19900014101](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19900014101). Authors **Curtis West, Sally Chamberlain, Neftali Pagan and Robert Stevens**. This is a **separate** report, not the same author group as the 1988 study. A [NASA-indexed conference paper](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/search.jsp?R=19940004537) also lists West, Chamberlain, Stevens and Pagan. **Do not merge the reports or their mission-duration assumptions.**

**Delivery questions:** autonomous operation, deceleration/capture versus flyby, payload protection and power over decades. Neither NASA report is a biological-delivery mission proposal.
